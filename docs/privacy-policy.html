Privacy Policy

AI Goodbye

Last Updated: January 11, 2026

Our Commitment: AI Goodbye is designed with privacy as its core principle. Your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Overview

AI Goodbye ("the App") is an offline artificial intelligence assistant that runs entirely on your iPhone. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our App.

Data Collection

We do not collect any data. AI Goodbye is designed to operate completely offline, and we have no servers that receive your information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not collect usage analytics
  • We do not use tracking technologies
  • We do not collect device identifiers
  • We do not use advertising networks

On-Device Processing

All AI processing happens locally on your device:

  • Conversations: Your chats with the AI are processed and stored only on your device
  • Documents: PDFs, images, and other files you upload are analyzed locally
  • Voice: Speech recognition uses Apple's on-device speech recognition
  • Knowledge Base: Any personal data you choose to include (calendar, health, reminders) is accessed and processed locally

Optional Cloud AI Services

The App offers optional integration with third-party AI services (ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI). If you choose to use these features:

  • You provide your own API keys directly to these services
  • Your queries are sent directly to the respective service providers
  • We do not proxy, store, or have access to these communications
  • These services have their own privacy policies that govern your use

Use of cloud AI services is entirely optional. The App functions fully without them.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync:

  • Your conversation history is synced through Apple's iCloud service
  • This data is encrypted by Apple and subject to Apple's privacy policy
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data
  • iCloud sync is opt-in and can be disabled at any time

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of inadvertently collecting children's information.

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features
  • Folder passwords in the App use local encryption
  • We recommend using a device passcode and Face ID/Touch ID for additional security

Your Rights

Because we do not collect your data:

  • There is no data to request, correct, or delete from our systems
  • You have complete control over all data stored locally on your device
  • You can delete the App at any time to remove all associated data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
